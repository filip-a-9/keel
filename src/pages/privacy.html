Privacy

This site does not track you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. It is a set of static pages hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may keep its own server logs; Keel cannot see them and does not receive them.

If you email Keel

The contact is {{email}}. Keel receives your email address and whatever you write, and uses it only to reply and, if you order, to run and deliver your audit. Keel does not sell or share it, and does not add you to a mailing list.

Email is handled through a Gmail account, so Google processes the messages as the mail provider.

Payment

Payment is requested through Payoneer, which processes payment details under its own privacy policy. Keel does not see card or bank details.

Who is responsible

Keel AI Visibility is operated by an individual based in Serbia. There is no registered company. To ask what is held about you, or to have it deleted, email the address above.

Audit data

An audit queries AI assistants with questions about a brand and its market. It does not collect personal data about your customers. Your intake answers and the audit files are kept to deliver the report and answer follow-up questions.
