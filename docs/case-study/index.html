Case study: Teamwork.com, October 2026

Independent study of Teamwork.com, October 2026. Not commissioned by or affiliated with Teamwork.com. Teamwork.com has not seen or approved this page.

Teamwork.com is mentioned in most ChatGPT answers about project management for agencies. The clear exception is resource planning, where it appeared in 25% of answers and Asana in 85%.

Method

  • 280 answers from the ChatGPT API (model gpt-5.6-luna, with web search, US location).
  • 6 question themes, each asked in 4 wordings (24 prompts in all).
  • Compared against Asana, monday.com, ClickUp, Trello and Notion.
  • Every answer used the web search tool at least once. All 280 answers were usable; none failed or were excluded.
  • Sampling continued until each theme's 95% interval was within ±10 points. The resource-planning theme needed 80 answers; the other five needed 40 each.

Each rate has a 95% interval in the full report. For example, Teamwork.com's 25% on resource planning has an interval of 17% to 35% on 80 answers, and Asana's 85% has 76% to 91%. Those intervals do not overlap, so the gap is not noise.

Headline finding

Teamwork.com is strong almost everywhere: 90% or more on five of the six themes. The actionable gap is resource planning: 25% for Teamwork.com against 85% for Asana and 68% for monday.com.

The results also show how much the answer depends on the question. On the "client projects and billable hours" theme, Teamwork.com's rate across its four wordings ran from 60% to 100%. One wording alone would have given a very different picture.

Cited pages and domains

Pages on teamwork.com were cited in 135 of 280 answers (48%, interval 42% to 54%). The most-cited Teamwork.com pages were its agencies page (29 answers), its pricing page (27) and a support article on standard project budgets (20). A support article on workload planning was cited in 1 answer, and a blog post on resource-planning tools in 5.

The third-party domains cited most often, which are neither Teamwork.com nor a listed competitor, were smartsheet.com (76 answers), wrike.com (54), zapier.com (32), float.com (29), getharvest.com (29) and capterra.com (28). For a gap like resource planning, these are the sites where coverage would be worth examining. More on reading citations.

Limitations

  • The bare word "Teamwork" was not counted, because it is an ordinary English word. Answers that wrote only "Teamwork" are missed, so the figures slightly undercount.
  • The answers come from the ChatGPT API with web search, not the consumer ChatGPT app, and may differ from what an individual user sees.
  • One model, one location (US), one day (30 October 2026). Rates can move.
  • Because runs were added until the target was met, the intervals are approximate.
  • Only whether a brand is mentioned is measured, not its position in the answer.
  • The study shows what the assistant said, not why.
