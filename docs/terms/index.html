Terms

Short terms for Keel AI Visibility audits. If anything here is unclear, email keel.agents@gmail.com before you order.

What you buy

One audit, Snapshot (£249) or Full (£749), as described on the offer page. Both are one-off. Delivery is an HTML report and a PDF, sent by email.

Payment

After we confirm your order, we email a Payoneer payment request in GBP. The full amount is paid up front. Work starts when payment arrives, and the working-day count starts then.

Revisions

One round of revisions to the report text and to the question themes is included.

Founding offer

The first 3 customers get 40% off: Snapshot £149 and Full £449. In exchange, they give permission to publish an anonymised case study. The offer is limited to the first 3 customers.

Refunds

  • Full refund if you cancel by email before answer collection starts.
  • No refund once answers have been collected.

We email you to say when answer collection starts.

White label

White label is included at no extra cost. On request, the report header carries your agency name and Keel is not mentioned in the report.

What the audit is

A measurement of what ChatGPT (API with web search) and, for Full, Claude (Claude Code web search) said at the time of collection. It is not the consumer ChatGPT or claude.ai app, and answers may differ from what an individual user sees. We do not guarantee rankings, mentions or traffic. See the method.
