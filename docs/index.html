SaaS companies and online brands

If you sell software or products online, the same audit shows whether buyers who ask an AI assistant for recommendations hear your name, and which sites the answer leans on. The worked SaaS example walks through a real study. You can order directly: Snapshot or Full.

Why trust the numbers

Detection is deterministic: a brand counts as mentioned when its name, an agreed alias or one of its domains appears in the answer. No model grades another model. Sampling continues until each theme's rate is within about ±10 percentage points. The method page has the detail, and the sample report shows the output.

Limits, stated up front. Answers come from the ChatGPT API with web search and, for the Full audit, from Claude through Claude Code's web search. That is not the consumer ChatGPT or claude.ai app, and answers may differ from what an individual user sees. Nothing here guarantees a ranking.
