The 8 intake questions

Please keep the numbers (1., 2. and so on) at the start of each answer in your reply. They are how the answers are read.

  1. Brand name.
  2. Other names / aliases: product names, common misspellings.
  3. Website domains that belong to the brand: website, docs, blog, shop.
  4. Up to 5 competitors, each with its domain.
  5. Main country.
  6. The questions buyers ask, in their own words: 6 for Snapshot, 10 to 15 for Full. Unsure? Write a few and say so; Keel will propose the rest for your approval.
  7. Pages you most want cited by AI answers.
  8. Tier: Snapshot or Full.

One note on names: ordinary English words are a judgement call. The Teamwork.com study did not count the bare word "Teamwork", so list any such names in question 2 and say how you want them treated.

The template also has an optional line for a white-label agency name, which goes in the report header. White label is free.
